# Solstice Health Analytics – Change Management Policy

## Project Overview
This project presents a Change Management Policy for Solstice Health Analytics, designed to ensure changes to IT systems, infrastructure, and services are properly requested, assessed, authorised, implemented, and reviewed.

The policy aims to protect organisational data, technology assets, information security and the availability of critical healthcare analytics services.

## Policy Scope
The policy applies to:
- Employees, contractors, consultants and third-party personnel.
- All organisational locations where IT resources are used.
- All IT systems, resources and connected devices.

## Key Control Requirements
The policy establishes the following controls:

- **Change Requests:** Changes must be formally recorded before implementation.
- **Risk Assessment:** Evaluate business impact, security, data protection, availability and system dependencies.
- **Change Approval:** Obtain authorisation based on change risk and classification, including CAB oversight.
- **Testing and Implementation:** Implement approved changes within agreed windows, with appropriate testing and rollback arrangements.
- **Emergency Changes:** Use an expedited approval process, with retrospective recording and review.
- **Post-Implementation Review:** Confirm outcomes, document issues and record lessons learned.
- **Exceptions and Compliance:** Document, risk-assess and approve policy exceptions.

## Key Gap and Implementation Considerations
The policy defines the required governance framework. Operational procedures and evidence should support the following areas:

| Area | Implementation Requirement |
|---|---|
| Change records | Maintain a central change register and complete request forms. |
| Risk assessment | Use a consistent change impact and risk assessment template. |
| Approval | Maintain approval records and a clear authority matrix. |
| Testing | Retain test plans, results and evidence of user acceptance where applicable. |
| Emergency changes | Maintain an emergency change log and retrospective approval/review evidence. |
| Rollback | Document recovery plans and demonstrate that they are viable. |
| Post-implementation | Record review outcomes, incidents and corrective actions. |
| Monitoring | Report change success, failures, unauthorised changes and overdue reviews. |

*These are practical implementation considerations, not confirmed audit findings in the policy report.*

## Roles and Responsibilities
- **Change Manager:** Maintains the process, change register, schedule and management reporting.
- **Change Requester:** Provides accurate request, impact and implementation information.
- **CAB:** Reviews risks, business impact and approval recommendations.
- **IT Operations:** Performs technical assessment, testing and implementation.
- **CISO:** Reviews security implications and significant risks.
- **System and Business Owners:** Confirm business requirements and operational impacts.
